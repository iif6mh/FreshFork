Frequently Asked Questions

What is FreshFork?

FreshFork is a community-driven app for discovering, sharing, and saving healthy recipes tailored to your lifestyle—whether you're vegan, low-carb, or just eating clean.

What kinds of recipes can I find on FreshFork?

You'll find a wide variety of wholesome recipes—from plant-based meals and high-protein snacks to gluten-free desserts and seasonal dishes.

Can I upload my own recipes?

Absolutely! FreshFork lets you share your own healthy creations with easy steps, including ingredients, instructions, and photos.

Is there support for dietary restrictions?

Yes, you can filter and tag recipes based on preferences like vegan, keto, dairy-free, and more.

Do I need an account to use the app?

You can browse as a guest, but to save, share, or upload recipes, you'll need to create a free account.
